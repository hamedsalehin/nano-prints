<h2>What Is Duratrans and How Does It Work?</h2>
<p>Duratrans is a polyester-base backlit photographic film with an optical coating optimized for transmitted light viewing. Unlike standard opaque print media that reflects ambient light, duratrans film is designed to transmit light from an internal light source—typically fluorescent, LED, or cold-cathode illumination inside a lightbox cabinet. This transmitted-light viewing condition creates dramatically different color appearance compared to reflective prints:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Color Gamut Expansion:</strong> Backlit viewing reveals a significantly wider perceived color gamut than reflective surfaces. Reds appear more vivid, blues appear deeper, and gradients show smoother transitions across the full tonal range.</li>
  <li><strong>Luminance Depth:</strong> Backlit images achieve luminance levels of 500–2,000 candelas per square meter (cd/m²), far exceeding the 100–300 cd/m² typical of reflective prints. This creates the perception of three-dimensional depth and photographic realism that makes product photography in lightboxes so compelling.</li>
  <li><strong>Shadow Preservation:</strong> While bright areas in a backlit image appear luminous and vivid, shadow areas retain detail that would appear as blocked-up blacks in reflective prints. This expanded dynamic range is particularly valuable for food photography, automotive imagery, and landscape backgrounds.</li>
</ul>
