<h2>Types of Company Signs We Fabricate</h2>
<p>Nano Signs offers a comprehensive range of company signage solutions designed to meet the specific needs of every business type and location:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Illuminated Channel Letters:</strong> Individual three-dimensional letters mounted directly to building facades, available in front-lit, halo-lit, and combination configurations. Channel letters dramatically increase nighttime visibility and brand recognition, making them the preferred company sign solution for retail and restaurant businesses throughout Fort Lauderdale, Sunrise, and Plantation.</li>
  <li><strong>Monument Signs:</strong> Ground-level masonry, aluminum, or HDU foam monument signs for office parks, retail plazas, hotels, and professional services buildings. Monument signs create an authoritative first impression at property entrances and can accommodate LED tenant directories for multi-tenant properties.</li>
  <li><strong>Building-Mounted Panel Signs:</strong> Internally illuminated single-face and double-face aluminum cabinet signs, also known as lightbox signs, available in custom sizes for fast food chains, service stations, and retail storefronts.</li>
  <li><strong>Lobby and Interior Signs:</strong> Precision-cut aluminum, acrylic, and brass dimensional letters, ADA-compliant room identification plaques, wayfinding systems, and branded wall graphics for professional office environments.</li>
  <li><strong>Pylon and Pole Signs:</strong> High-visibility roadside company identification signs mounted on steel poles, ideal for commercial corridors with high-speed traffic along US-1, Commercial Boulevard, and Oakland Park Boulevard.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Materials and Construction Methods</h3>
<p>Nano Signs uses only commercial-grade materials in company sign fabrication. Aluminum channel letter returns are formed from 0.063-inch marine-grade aluminum sheet, TIG-welded at seams and finished with UV-stable powder coating. Acrylic faces are laser-cut from 3/16-inch to 1/4-inch cast acrylic and second-surface printed or painted for maximum UV resistance. All LED illumination components are UL-listed and mounted with waterproof sealing to prevent moisture intrusion in South Florida's high-humidity environment.</p>

<h2>Company Sign Permitting and Installation</h2>
<p>Installing a permanent company sign in Broward County requires a building permit, electrical permit for illuminated signs, and compliance review for height, setback, and square footage limits defined in local zoning ordinances. Nano Signs manages the complete permit application process on behalf of our clients, coordinating directly with Broward County Building and Zoning, Fort Lauderdale Development Services, and other municipal authorities as required.</p>
<p>Our licensed installation crew operates a bucket truck for elevated installations and uses certified structural anchoring systems compliant with Florida Building Code § 3107 High-Velocity Hurricane Zone wind-load requirements. We carry full general liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage, providing complete peace of mind for property owners and commercial tenants.</p>
