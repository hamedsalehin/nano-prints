<h3>2. Commercial Aluminum vs. Heavy-Duty Steel Truss Frames</h3>
<ul>

  <li><strong>Hexagonal Aluminum Truss Frames:</strong> Features lightweight 40mm or 50mm hexagonal legs. Delivers maximum structural strength against sudden ocean wind gusts while weighing 30% less, making it effortless to load into vehicle trunks and roll across event grounds.</li>

</ul>
