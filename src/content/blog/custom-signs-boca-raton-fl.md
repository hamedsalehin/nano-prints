<h3>1. Reverse Halo-Lit Channel Letters</h3>
<p>The gold standard for luxury retail storefronts and corporate offices, reverse halo-lit (reverse channel) letters feature welded aluminum faces and returns with clear polycarbonate backings. Mounted on concealed standoffs off the wall, internal warm-white LEDs project a soft, sophisticated halo of light onto the facade behind the lettering, creating a dramatic, upscale silhouette without harsh direct glare.</p>

<h3>2. Precision-Machined Solid Metal & Acrylic Dimensional Letters</h3>
<p>For executive boardrooms, corporate reception lobbies, and exterior facades, we mill solid brass, bronze, marine-grade 316 stainless steel, and cast acrylic panels. Available in brushed, satin, polished, or oxidized finishes, dimensional lettering delivers undeniable visual permanence and corporate prestige.</p>

<h3>3. Architectural Post-and-Panel & Monument Systems</h3>

<h2>Navigating City of Boca Raton Sign Codes and Architectural Review</h2>
<p>The City of Boca Raton enforces one of the strictest municipal sign ordinances in the state of Florida. Regulations strictly regulate total sign square footage, prohibited sign types (such as exposed raceways, flashing neon, or garish color schemes), and mandate comprehensive Architectural Review Commission (ARC) approvals for exterior modifications:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Concealed Fasteners & Flush Mounting:</strong> Boca Raton code mandates that electrical raceways, wiring, and mounting transformers remain fully concealed behind the building parapet or recessed into the facade wall.</li>
  <li><strong>Color & Typography Cohesion:</strong> Sign designs must integrate cohesively with the overall building architecture and neighboring retail facades in terms of color palette and scale.</li>
  <li><strong>Florida Building Code Wind-Load Compliance:</strong> All exterior structural signage must be engineered to withstand 150+ MPH wind loads with signed and sealed engineering drawings from a Florida-licensed Professional Engineer (PE).</li>
</ul>
<p>Nano Signs manages the entire compliance process from start to finish, drafting compliant architectural shop drawings, representing clients at design reviews, and securing all required municipal permits.</p>
