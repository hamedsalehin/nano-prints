<hr />

<h2>Comparing the 3 Popular Exhibition Styles</h2>

<h3>1. Standard Draped Tablecloths (Loose Throw)</h3>
<p>The timeless commercial classic. This throw drapes casually over the top and four sides of the table with natural folding corners.</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Table Size Flexibility:</strong> Forgiving fabric dimensions allow an 8ft throw to work comfortably on a 6ft table when folded underneath.</li>
  <li><strong>Open-Back (3-Sided) vs. Closed-Back (4-Sided):</strong> An open-back design leaves the rear side accessible, allowing booth representatives to sit comfortably behind the table and store inventory, literature boxes, and personal bags out of sight.</li>
</ul>

<h3>2. Fitted Tablecloths</h3>
<p>Tailored specifically with crisp vertical corner seams sewn to match exact commercial table dimensions (6ft: 72"x30"x29" or 8ft: 96"x30"x29").</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Clean Architectural Lines:</strong> Provides a sharp, rectangular silhouette with zero loose fabric bunching at the corners.</li>
  <li><strong>Wind Resistance:</strong> Does not flap or shift, making it a great option for outdoor corporate tent events and university campus recruitment fairs.</li>
</ul>

<h3>3. Modern Stretch Spandex Table Covers</h3>
<p>The ultra-modern corporate standard. Crafted from elastic polyester-spandex blend that contours snugly around the entire table, secured with reinforced foot pockets under each table leg.</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>100% Wrinkle-Free Display:</strong> Tension eliminates every single fold or crease instantly upon installation. Ideal for busy road warriors who travel with tablecloths stuffed into suitcases.</li>
  <li><strong>Sleek Contour Aesthetic:</strong> Highly popular for tech startups, luxury brand activations, high-end DJ booths, and gala catering stations.</li>
</ul>
