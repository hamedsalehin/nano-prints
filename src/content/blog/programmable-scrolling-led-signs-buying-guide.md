<h2>Key Technical Specifications to Evaluate Before Buying</h2>

<h3>1. Pixel Pitch (Resolution)</h3>
<p>Pixel pitch indicates the center-to-center distance between individual LED clusters (measured in millimeters):</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>P2.5 & P3.9 Modules:</strong> Ideal for indoor retail showrooms, bank lobbies, and close-range window displays (viewing distance: 6 to 15 feet).</li>
  <li><strong>P5 & P6 Modules:</strong> The sweet spot for sidewalk storefronts, monument signs, and strip plaza exteriors (viewing distance: 15 to 45 feet).</li>
  <li><strong>P10 Modules:</strong> Engineered for roadside pylon signs, highway billboards, and elevated commercial facades where viewers travel at 45+ mph (viewing distance: 40 to 150+ feet).</li>
</ul>

<h3>2. Daylight Visibility & Brightness (Nits)</h3>
<p>South Florida sunshine is unforgiving. An indoor screen (typically 800 to 1,500 nits) is completely unreadable in direct sunlight. Commercial outdoor scrolling LED signs manufactured by <strong>Nano Signs</strong> output between <strong>6,000 and 8,500 Nits</strong> of high-intensity SMD illumination, backed by automatic photocell ambient light sensors that dim brightness at night to comply with local Broward municipal light-spill ordinances.</p>

<h3>3. Weatherproofing: High-Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ) & IP65 Enclosures</h3>
<p>Between torrential tropical storms, salt-spray corrosion, and extreme summer humidity, LED electronics demand military-grade protection. Our digital displays feature aluminum unibody cabinets, silicone-conformal-coated PCB drive cards, and front-and-rear <strong>IP65 sealed gaskets</strong> to prevent water intrusion and overheating.</p>
