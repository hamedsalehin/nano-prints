<h3>Products Requiring Standard Lead Times</h3>
<p>While we pride ourselves on fast turnaround, certain complex products require additional production time regardless of rush service: illuminated channel letters (5–10 business days for fabrication), monument signs (2–4 weeks including permit processing), vehicle wraps (3–5 days for printing and installation), and custom architectural signs with dimensional letters require manufacturing time that cannot be significantly compressed. We always communicate lead times clearly upfront so our clients can plan accordingly.</p>
