<h2>The Second-Surface Printing & Face Mounting Technique</h2>
<p>Achieving photographic perfection on acrylic requires microscopic dot precision and optical materials:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Cast Optical Acrylic:</strong> We utilize ultra-pure cast acrylic sheets (1/8-inch, 1/4-inch, or 1/2-inch thickness) with superior light transmission over standard glass.</li>
  <li><strong>Second-Surface UV Printing:</strong> Artwork is printed directly onto the backside of the clear acrylic, allowing light to refract naturally through the panel.</li>
  <li><strong>Opaque White Barrier Coating:</strong> A high-density white ink layer is deposited behind the image to reflect light forward and enhance vibrancy.</li>
  <li><strong>Diamond-Polished Beveled Edges:</strong> Panel perimeters are machine-polished to crystal-clear optical perfection, creating a refined borderless look.</li>
  <li><strong>Architectural Standoff Hardware:</strong> Brushed stainless steel, matte black, or brass standoffs suspend the acrylic 1 inch away from your wall.</li>
</ul>
