<h3>ADA Compliance for Indoor Signs</h3>
<p>The ADA (Americans with Disabilities Act) mandates specific sign standards for all public-facing commercial facilities. Non-compliant signs can result in complaints, enforcement actions, and costly retrofit projects. Key ADA requirements for indoor signs include: Grade 2 Braille on all permanent room identification signs, characters must be raised 1/32 inch from the sign face, non-glare finish required on all sign faces, 70% contrast minimum between characters and background, mounting height of 60 inches to centerline on the latch side of doors, and pictogram symbols must meet specific size and proportion requirements. Nano Signs produces ADA-compliant signs that meet all current 2010 ADA Standards requirements, with documentation available for accessibility compliance verification.</p>
