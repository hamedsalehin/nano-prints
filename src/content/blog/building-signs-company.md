<h3>Structural Engineering for Florida Building Signs</h3>
<p>Every building sign installation in Broward County requires a building permit and structural compliance review. Florida Building Code § 3107 mandates specific wind-load design for all exterior signs. Our building signs company prepares engineered drawings by licensed structural engineers, specifying anchor type, embedment depth, and connection design for every installation. For building-mounted signs, we analyze the host structure's construction type (concrete masonry unit, concrete tilt-up, steel frame with metal stud infill, wood frame) and specify through-bolts, lag bolts, or adhesive anchor systems appropriate for the design loads and substrate conditions. All electrical connections for illuminated building signs are made by licensed electricians to UL 48 electric sign standards.</p>

<h2>Building Sign Installation Process</h2>
<p>Our building sign installation process begins with a pre-installation site survey—visiting your property to photograph the building facade, measure the sign area, assess substrate construction, identify power source proximity for illuminated signs, and verify clearances from obstructions. Following survey, we prepare engineered drawings for permit submission, fabricate sign components in our Oakland Park shop, and schedule installation upon permit approval.
