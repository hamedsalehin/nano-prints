<h2>Key Factors to Consider When You Need a Sign Made</h2>
<p>Understanding these critical variables ensures your sign project succeeds within your budget:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Indoor vs. Outdoor Environment:</strong> Exterior signs require rust-proof aluminum, UV-cured inks, and hurricane-rated wind engineering, while interior signs focus on fine architectural finishes.</li>
  <li><strong>Illumination Requirements:</strong> Determine whether daytime visibility is sufficient, or if your business requires 24/7 exposure via front-lit or halo-lit LED channel letters.</li>
  <li><strong>Viewing Distance & Speed:</strong> A roadside sign on a 45 MPH avenue requires bold, 12-inch or larger lettering, whereas pedestrian storefronts can utilize intricate typography.</li>
  <li><strong>Municipal Permitting:</strong> Exterior building signs require approval from local city halls (Oakland Park, Fort Lauderdale, Pompano Beach); our team manages this process completely.</li>
</ul>
