<h2>What to Look for in Commercial Sign Vendors</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>In-House Production Capability:</strong> Sign vendors who own and operate their production equipment can control quality, turnaround, and cost more effectively than brokers who outsource manufacturing. Ask potential vendors specifically what equipment they operate in-house.</li>
  <li><strong>Material and Technical Expertise:</strong> Experienced sign vendors understand the technical differences between material types, can advise on appropriate substrate selection for specific applications, and can troubleshoot production issues that require technical knowledge beyond basic printing.</li>
  <li><strong>Local Presence and Accountability:</strong> Sign vendors with a local physical presence are accountable in ways that online-only vendors are not. When problems arise, you can visit their facility, speak face-to-face with production staff, and resolve issues without navigating remote customer service systems.</li>
  <li><strong>License and Insurance Verification:</strong> For sign installation services, verify that vendors carry current Florida State contractor licenses, general liability insurance (minimum $1M per occurrence), and workers' compensation coverage. These credentials are non-negotiable for commercial property installations.</li>
  <li><strong>References and Portfolio:</strong> Established sign vendors can provide references from comparable local businesses and show a portfolio of completed projects that demonstrates their range and quality level.</li>
</ul>
