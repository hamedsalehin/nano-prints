<h2>What Full-Service Sign Making Involves</h2>
<p>Many business owners don't realize how many specialized disciplines contribute to a professionally fabricated commercial sign. At Nano Signs, our in-house team covers every phase:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Design & Pre-Press:</strong> Our graphic designers create vector artwork optimized for large-format output, perform Pantone PMS color matching, and submit drawings for municipal permit applications when required.</li>
  <li><strong>Substrate Fabrication:</strong> Our CNC router cuts aluminum composite material, cast acrylic, PVC Sintra, Dibond, and HDU (High-Density Urethane) foam to precise dimensions and contoured shapes.</li>
  <li><strong>Wide-Format Printing:</strong> UV flatbed printers deposit fade-resistant, weather-proof inks directly onto rigid panels or flexible vinyl materials in widths up to 10 feet.</li>
  <li><strong>Illuminated Sign Assembly:</strong> UL-listed LED modules are assembled into aluminum channel letter returns, lightbox cabinets, and neon-flex installations with certified electrical components compliant with UL 48 electric sign standards.</li>
  <li><strong>Licensed Installation:</strong> Our bucket-truck crew handles structural mounting, through-bolt anchoring on masonry and steel substrates, and certified electrical connections for illuminated signs.</li>
</ul>
